What an FP&A analyst does
  • Budgeting: plans how much the business will earn and spend.
  • Forecasting: updates the plan as the year goes on.
  • Variance analysis: explains why actual results differ from the plan.
  • Reporting: turns numbers into clear insights for managers.

Each step in my roadmap builds one of these skills.
